# Product Context — PawPrints AI

## What
AI pet portrait generator. Upload a pet photo, choose an art style, get a high-res portrait in under 60 seconds.

## Audience
Pet owners (25-45, primarily female) who treat their pets as family. Secondary: gift buyers.

## Pricing
- **Free**: 1 portrait, standard resolution
- **Pro**: $8.99/month — unlimited portraits, high-res (2048x2048), no watermarks, all styles + new monthly styles

## Art Styles
Renaissance, Anime, Superhero, Watercolor, Pop Art. New styles added monthly for Pro members.

## Design System
- **Primary**: Warm Amber #D97706 (HSL 38 90% 44%)
- **Surface**: Warm Cream #FDF8F3
- **Fonts**: DM Serif Display (display) + DM Sans (body)
- **Mode**: Light default, warm off-white backgrounds
- **Aesthetic**: Gallery-like, warm, generous whitespace, portraits are the star

## Key Pages
- `/` — Landing page (Navbar, Hero, Social Proof, Style Showcase, How It Works, Features Bento, Pricing, Testimonials, FAQ, CTA, Footer)
- `/login`, `/signup`, `/forgot-password` — Auth pages
- `/dashboard` — Upload, style select, generate, download, portrait gallery

## Copy Rules
- Lead with emotion, follow with function
- Be specific about styles ("Renaissance oil painting" not "artistic style")
- Use gentle humor about pets
- Handle memorial portraits with sensitivity
- Never say: algorithm, neural network, platform, leverage, optimize, cutting-edge, seamless
