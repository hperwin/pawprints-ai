# PawPrints AI — AI Pet Portrait Generator

## Audience
Pet owners who love their pets like family. Gift buyers (birthdays, holidays, memorials). Pet influencers. Emotionally driven, not technically driven.

## Voice
Warm, playful, emotional. The pet is the star, not the AI. Use "your pet", "your best friend", "their personality." Never clinical or corporate.

## Design
- Font: DM Serif Display (headings) + DM Sans (body) — NO Inter
- Color: Warm Amber #D97706 — NO purple/indigo
- Mode: Light default (warm, gallery aesthetic)
- Feel: Art gallery for pets, not a tech tool

## Copy Rules
- NEVER use tech jargon ("AI-powered", "neural network", "machine learning")
- ALWAYS lead with emotion ("Turn your pet into a masterpiece", "The portrait they deserve")
- Reference specific styles by name (Renaissance, anime, watercolor) — these are what users search for

## Key Competitors
DreamPets, Fotor pet portraits, ImagineMe, Purina AI portraits — research these before any feature work.
