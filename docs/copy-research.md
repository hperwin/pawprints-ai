# PawPrints AI -- Copy Research

*Compiled: 2026-03-27*

## How Pet Owners Talk About Their Pets

### Language patterns from Reddit, Etsy reviews, and competitor testimonials

- "fur baby" / "fur babies" -- extremely common, used unironically
- "good boy" / "good girl" -- universal for dogs
- "my baby" / "my little guy" / "my best friend"
- "part of the family" / "family member"
- "crossed the rainbow bridge" -- universal euphemism for pet death
- "gotcha day" -- adoption anniversary
- "dog mom" / "cat dad" / "pet parent"
- "zoomies" / "derp face" / "blep" -- playful pet behavior terms
- "rescue" -- worn as a badge of pride
- "they have so much personality"
- "I don't deserve this dog"

### Emotional triggers that drive purchases

1. **Pride** -- "Look at my beautiful dog. Everyone should see this."
2. **Love** -- "I want to celebrate how much this pet means to me."
3. **Grief** -- "I need something to remember them by."
4. **Gifting joy** -- "This will make them cry happy tears."
5. **Social sharing** -- "My Instagram followers are going to lose it."
6. **Novelty/humor** -- "My cat as a Renaissance duke? Yes please."

### What makes someone buy a pet portrait?

**Trigger moments:**
- Seeing someone else's pet portrait on social media (most common)
- Holiday gift shopping, running out of ideas
- Pet's birthday or adoption anniversary
- Pet passing away or becoming elderly
- Moving into a new home (empty walls)
- Just adopted a new pet (celebration)

**Buying concerns (in order of importance):**
1. "Will it actually look like my pet?" (likeness accuracy)
2. "Is the quality good enough to print and frame?"
3. "How long does it take?"
4. "Can I get a refund if I don't like it?"
5. "Can I include multiple pets?"
6. "What photo should I use?"

## Real Testimonial Language (from competitor reviews)

These are the kinds of phrases real customers use. Copy should match this register:

**Positive:**
- "I genuinely gasped when I saw it"
- "It's hanging above our fireplace"
- "Best gift I've ever given"
- "She cried when she opened it"
- "It captures his personality perfectly"
- "My coworkers keep asking where I got it"
- "I've ordered five more for Christmas gifts"
- "The likeness is incredible"

**Negative (competitor complaints to address):**
- "Looked like a generic [breed], not MY dog"
- "The colors were way off"
- "Looked like it was printed from a home printer"
- "Not worth the price for what I got"
- "Took forever and I still wasn't happy"
- "No option for multiple pets"
- "The AI clearly didn't understand my pet's markings"

## Copy Direction for PawPrints AI

### Tone
Warm, confident, slightly playful. Not corporate. Not overly cutesy. Think "your clever friend who happens to love pets" -- not "generic pet brand voice."

### Key messages to hit
1. **Likeness promise**: "Your actual pet, not a generic breed."
2. **Speed**: "60 seconds. Not 6 weeks."
3. **Print quality**: "Frame-worthy. Museum-quality. Not a phone filter."
4. **Gift angle**: "The gift that makes pet lovers cry happy tears."
5. **Memorial angle**: "A beautiful way to remember them."
6. **Low risk**: "First one's free. No credit card."
7. **Variety**: "8 styles, from Renaissance royalty to watercolor dreams."

### Headlines to test
- "Turn your pet into a masterpiece" (current -- solid, keep testing)
- "Your pet, painted like they deserve"
- "The portrait your pet would commission if they could"
- "60 seconds from photo to frame-worthy art"
- "They're not just a pet. Give them art that proves it."

- "Create your pet's portrait"
