## Product Hunt Listing

### Tagline
Turn your pet's photo into frame-worthy art in 60 seconds.

### Description
PawPrints AI transforms your favorite pet photo into a stunning portrait -- watercolor, Renaissance, pop art, anime, memorial, and more. Upload a photo, pick a style, and download a print-ready portrait in under a minute.

**What makes PawPrints AI different:**

- **8 curated styles** tuned for print quality and likeness accuracy
- **60-second delivery** vs. days or weeks from traditional services
- **Print-ready downloads** at 300 DPI in standard frame sizes (5x7, 8x10, 11x14, 16x20)
- **Photo crop + zoom** to focus on your pet's face
- **12+ backgrounds** (studio, outdoor, fantasy, seasonal)
- **6 digital frame styles** (gold ornate, modern, rustic, floating, gallery mat)
- **Gift card creator** with personalized messages and unwrapping animation
- **Multi-pet portraits** -- combine 2-3 pets in one portrait
- **Style intensity slider** -- from realistic to boldly stylized
- **Before/after comparison** slider
- **Favorites gallery** with shareable public links
- **Memorial style** for pets who've crossed the rainbow bridge

**Pricing:** First portrait free. Pro: $8.99/mo unlimited (or $7.19/mo billed yearly).

### Maker Comment
We built PawPrints AI because we love our pets and we wanted their portraits to look like *them*, not a generic breed template.

The biggest complaint about existing pet portrait services? "It doesn't look like MY dog." We obsess over likeness accuracy -- your pet's specific markings, coloring, and expression.

Crown & Paw charges $50-$140 and takes a week. Commission artists charge $200+ and take weeks. We give you the first one free and the result takes 60 seconds.

Would love your feedback. Upload a photo of your pet and let us know if the likeness holds up.
